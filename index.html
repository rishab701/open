A little letter for you

Dear Arpita (my Khushi),

I heard you’re not feeling well, and honestly, it made me a little sad too. When you’re feeling low, everything feels quiet. Without you, the laughter and the nonsense are both missing.

Just remember one thing: you’re not alone. I’m right here, one message away. Rest a lot, and be gentle with yourself. I’ll take care of the rest. You just get better and come back soon.

So I made you this small corner of the internet. Below you’ll find a few lines of shayari, a little checklist of small things that help, and a lot of prayers.
