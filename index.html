Dear Bestie,

This is our first Diwali as friends, and honestly, it’s hard to believe we only started talking in January. It feels like I’ve known you for much longer than that.

Thank you for the late-night talks, the jokes that only make sense to us, and for showing up every time it mattered this year. You made this year a lot brighter than I expected.

So for our first Diwali, instead of forwarding a sparkly GIF on WhatsApp, I made you a small corner of the internet. Below are five diyas waiting for you. Light each one and it’ll hand you a few lines in Hinglish, the way we actually talk.

Here’s to the first of many. Happy Diwali, stay bright, stay you.
